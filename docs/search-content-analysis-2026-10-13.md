## Scope and method

“Lower ranking but appearing” is defined here as impressions greater than zero and an exported page average position greater than 10. All 48 qualifying pages were refreshed, plus the sports guide. Average position is a blended metric, not a fixed rank or proof that a page always appears on page two. No improvement in future rankings or clicks is claimed.

Queries and pages are separate aggregated lists. Targets below are **editorial intent matches**, not verified Google landing pages. Synonyms share a useful guide. Existing relevant pages that rank in the top ten overall are retained even where an individual query ranks worse. No duplicate keyword pages were created.
